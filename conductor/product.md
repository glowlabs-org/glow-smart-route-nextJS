# Initial Concept
A NextJS fullstack application for purchasing Glow ecosystem tokens and interacting with the Glow protocol through a simplified, gamified experience.

# Vision
To transform the complexity of the Glow protocol into a fun, engaging, and highly accessible experience. By leveraging gamification and dopamine loops, the app aims to onboard users regardless of their technical knowledge, making climate-positive investments as simple as playing a game.

# Target Audience
- **Sustainability-focused individuals:** People interested in high-impact climate action but who may be intimidated by the technicalities of blockchain and DeFi.
- **Retail crypto investors:** Users looking for a user-friendly entry point into the Glow ecosystem.
- **Community participants:** Users motivated by social proof, leaderboards, and transparent impact metrics.

# Core Goals
1. **Simplify Complexity:** Shield users from the underlying protocol mechanics with an intuitive and beautiful UI.
2. **Drive Impact:** Incentivize actions that grow the Glow ecosystem's climate impact through the "Impact Score."
3. **Engagement via Gamification:** Use rewards, streaks, and leaderboards to create a "sticky" user experience.
4. **Unified Interface:** Provide a central "Bento" dashboard for managing all interactions—from purchasing GLW to delegating to solar farms.

# Key Features
- **Impact Score:** A gamified points system that tracks and rewards user contributions (Emissions earned, Steering via sGCTL, and Vault bonuses).
- **GCTL Minting & Staking:** A streamlined flow for users to acquire governance power and direct solar infrastructure funding.
- **Launchpad & Mining Center:** A unified marketplace for purchasing solar miners (USDC) and delegating GLW tokens to fund new solar farms.
- **Bento Dashboard:** A modern, high-density visualization of the user's portfolio, rank, and weekly activity.
- **Impact Leaderboard:** Social competition based on Impact Points to drive community engagement.
- **Smart Payment Routing:** Inline multi-step swaps (ETH -> USDC -> USDG -> GLW) to allow users to pay with their preferred assets seamlessly.
- **Protocol Metrics:** Real-time transparency into the health and economic status of the Glow ecosystem.
