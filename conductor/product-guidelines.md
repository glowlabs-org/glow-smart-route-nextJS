# Product Guidelines

## 1. Visual Identity & Hierarchy
- **Transaction-First Architecture:** The primary layout must prioritize immediate accessibility to core actions. Quick-action buttons for "Buy GLW," "Stake GCTL," and "Launchpad" status should occupy the most prominent "above-the-fold" positions.
- **Fast & Fluid Layouts:** Adhere to the established 8-pt spacing system and typographic scale to ensure the interface feels snappy and professional.
- **Integrated Impact Metrics:** While transactions are primary, impact data (Watts, Trees, Homes) should be treated as high-value secondary information, often paired directly with action buttons to provide immediate "Why" context.

## 2. Voice & Tone
- **Urgent & Action-Oriented:** Use active, direct language that emphasizes momentum. 
- **Loss Aversion as a Motivator:** Clearly communicate the value of maintaining streaks and multipliers. (e.g., "Keep your 3x multiplier active—stake GCTL before the Sunday rollover.")
- **Simplified Technicals:** Shield users from jargon. Translate protocol mechanics into outcome-based language and helpful real-world analogies.

## 3. Interaction & Feedback ("Juice")
- **Subtle but Constant Feedback:** Prioritize non-intrusive micro-interactions that keep the UI feeling "alive" without slowing down the user.
- **Key Patterns:**
    - **Animated Values:** Always use number count-ups for changing balances or scores.
    - **Surge Glows:** Use subtle glow pulses (Glow Orange/Green) on progress bars and panel "ghosts" to signal activity.
    - **Haptic Reinforcement:** Implement distinct vibration patterns on mobile for successful signatures and milestone completions.
- **Speed Over Spectacle:** Animations should be fast (<= 300ms) to ensure the interface never feels sluggish.

## 4. Gamification Logic
- **Habit Loop Reinforcement:** The UI must clearly signal "Streak Status" and "Next Goal" at all times.
- **The "Solar Collector" Journey:** Frame progress around the permanent collection of panels. Use visual metaphors (wireframes/liquid fills) to leverage the Goal-Gradient effect.
